How fast is your Seattle bus?

I compare morning, midday, and evening speeds on ten Seattle-serving Metro routes over 37 fall weekdays.

The interactive scorecard shows how speeds vary by route and time of day, with a map and a closer look at Denny Way. The speed estimates include time spent at passenger stops.

I used fall 2024 ridership data and looked for routes with consistent observation coverage. The analysis covers ten selected routes. The Route 8 observations come from after the bus lane work, so they describe post-installation conditions.
