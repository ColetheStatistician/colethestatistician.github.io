Nolan Cole

Biostatistics PhD Student

Department of Biostatistics, University of Washington

I am a PhD student in the Department of Biostatistics at the University of Washington in Seattle. I develop debiased machine learning methods for causal inference with Dr. Marco Carone.

Previously, I worked with Dr. Arkajyoti Saha and Dr. Daniela Witten to develop selective inference methods for high-dimensional data. I also worked with Dr. Pamela Shaw at the Kaiser Permanente Health Research Institute to develop research algorithms to handle measurement error in compositional data. In 2026, I concluded my time as a visiting graduate research fellow with Dr. Arjun Bhattacharya in the Department of Epidemiology at MD Anderson, where I applied causal inference methods to transcriptomic data from diverse tissue types.

I earned my bachelor’s degree in statistics. As an undergraduate, I researched rare-event meta-analysis methods with Dr. Brinley Zabriskie and analyzed epigenomic data with Dr. Steve Johnson. In 2020, I was a visiting undergraduate research fellow in Dr. Peter Kharchenko’s group at Harvard Medical School’s Department of Biomedical Informatics, where I contributed to developing methods for spatial transcriptomics data. In 2021, I studied distal gene regulation by non-coding RNAs in breast cancer using eQTLs and mediation analysis with Dr. Bogdan Pasaniuc at UCLA.
