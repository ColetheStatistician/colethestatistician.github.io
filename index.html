Nolan Cole

Biostatistics PhD Student

Department of Biostatistics, University of Washington

I am a PhD student in the Department of Biostatistics at the University of Washington in Seattle. I develop debiased machine learning methods for causal inference with Dr. Marco Carone.

Previously, I worked with Dr. Arkajyoti Saha and Dr. Daniela Witten to develop selective inference methods. I’m also interested in statistical genetics. At UCLA, I studied distal gene regulation by non-coding RNAs in breast cancer using eQTLs and mediation analysis with Dr. Arjun Bhattacharya and Dr. Bogdan Pasaniuc.

I earned my bachelor’s degree in statistics at Brigham Young University in Provo, Utah. As an undergraduate, I worked on meta-analysis with Dr. Brinley Zabriskie and epigenetics with Dr. Steve Johnson. In 2020, I was a visiting undergraduate research fellow in Dr. Peter Kharchenko’s group at Harvard Medical School’s Department of Biomedical Informatics.
